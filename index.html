Location

All Yoga Training's Bali courses take place in Jungut Batu on Nusa Lembongan, an island off Bali's southeast coast. Training is held in an open-air beachfront shala facing the ocean. Nusa Lembongan is reached by fast boat from Sanur on mainland Bali.

About

About All Yoga Training

All Yoga Training has focused on professional yoga teacher education since 2009. More than 3,500 students from around the world have completed its training programs.

The school's approach combines a strong Ashtanga Vinyasa foundation with practical teaching experience, anatomy, philosophy and individual feedback. Rather than focusing on retreat-style yoga experiences, its programs are structured around developing both personal practice and real teaching skills.

Bali is one of All Yoga Training's core training destinations, with courses held at its beachfront shala in Nusa Lembongan. Alongside its flagship 200-hour training, the school offers hybrid and advanced training pathways for students at different stages of their yoga education.

Complete course dates, curriculum, tuition and application information are available through the official All Yoga Training website.
