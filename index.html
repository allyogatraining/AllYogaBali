About the School

A practice-led approach to teacher training

All Yoga Training is a yoga teacher education school focused on practical, small-group learning. The Bali programs combine a structured asana foundation with teaching methodology, anatomy, philosophy, alignment and regular teaching practice.

The approach is built around Ashtanga Vinyasa, but the goal is broader than learning a sequence: students develop their personal practice, learn how to communicate clearly and gain experience teaching in front of others.

Current course dates, curriculum, tuition and applications are available at AllYogaTraining.com.

Location

Where is the Bali training held?

Courses take place in Jungut Batu on Nusa Lembongan, an island off Bali's southeast coast. The open-air shala sits by the beach, and the island is reached by fast boat from Sanur on mainland Bali.

Programs

Yoga teacher training programs in Bali

Choose from the 200-hour, hybrid 200-hour and 300-hour pathways. Course dates, full curriculum, tuition and application details are kept up to date on AllYogaTraining.com.

200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training

A foundational teacher training built around Ashtanga Vinyasa, with methodology, anatomy, philosophy, alignment and regular practice teaching.

Cohort cap: 24 students

Hybrid 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training

Online anatomy and philosophy paired with an in-person Bali intensive focused on asana, teaching methodology, feedback and practical teaching experience.

Cohort cap: 22 students

300-Hour Advanced Yoga Teacher Training

Advanced training for certified teachers, with continued development in practice and teaching, including Rocket Yoga, Yin and advanced study modules.

Cohort cap: 20 students
